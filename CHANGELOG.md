# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — first release (Minecraft 1.21.1, Iris)

The first public release of Redrock Visuals.

- **Lighting:** warm western sun and sunsets, moonlight, warm torchlight, soft shadows, cloud shadows, light through
  foliage, emissive blocks, handheld light and exposure that adapts to caves and the night.
- **Atmosphere:** volumetric dust fog with light shafts, morning mist and drifting dust; volumetric desert clouds;
  procedural sky with sun disk, sunset glow, moon halo and stars; border fog; underwater, lava and powder snow fog.
- **Water:** vanilla biome colors kept; real waves; refraction; depth absorption; Fresnel reflections (screen space,
  or ray traced through a voxel grid in the Ultra profile); soft sun glint; total internal reflection underwater.
- **Movement:** waving plants, leaves, water and lava, with wind gusts and stronger movement in the rain.
- **Image:** ACES tonemapping, bloom, adjustable western color grade, saturation, contrast and vignette.
- **Performance:** fog and clouds rendered at reduced resolution with depth-aware upscaling; Low, Medium, High and
  Ultra profiles.
- **Dimensions:** simple lighting and fog for the Nether and the End.
- Settings menu in English and Spanish.
- Open source under the MIT License.
